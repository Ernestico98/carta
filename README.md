## What it is

Carta is two tools in one system:

- **A visual design studio for menus.** Lay out pages, buttons, photos and navigation the way your brand looks, upright or sideways, with a live preview. Think of it as Figma for restaurant menus.
- **A control room for the tablets that show them.** Change a price, mark a dish sold out or swap a photo, press publish, and every tablet updates in seconds.

> This repository only holds the product presentation. The product itself is private.

## Design

![The Carta menu editor](assets/img/admin-editor.webp)

- Drag-and-drop editor with undo and redo, a layers panel and an image library
- Ready-made looks, plus your own fonts, colours, backgrounds and decorations
- Ready-made sections: covers, category buttons, dish cards, promotions, slideshows, opening hours
- Navigation bars as a side list, top tabs or a drawer
- Upright or sideways tablets: the editor previews the exact screen shape and the pages reflow
- A **Try it** mode to tap through the menu before publishing

![Ready-made looks](assets/img/admin-looks.webp)

## Your team, every day

Managers fix the menu from the tablet in their hand: a long press in a hidden corner, a PIN, a tap on the dish, publish. Every table updates in seconds.

- Prices, sold-out dishes, photos and descriptions in one place, with tags and bulk edits
- **Publish** once and every tablet updates
- Staff PINs: managers and kitchen edit the content, the design stays protected
- See which tablets are online and whether they show the latest menu; find, restart or update a tablet from the desk
- Tablets find the server by themselves over the restaurant's Wi-Fi, and keep the photos saved on the device
- Import and export a whole menu for backups or remote support

## How it works

1. **We design it with you.** Your brand, dishes and photos, laid out for upright or sideways tablets.
2. **We set up your tablets.** A small server runs in your restaurant and the tablets connect over your Wi-Fi.
3. **Your team runs it.** Staff update prices, sold-out dishes and photos and publish. We stay available for new looks, seasons and support.
